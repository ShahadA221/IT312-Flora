Our Mission :

At Flora, our mission is to create a welcoming community where a shared love for flowers becomes an opportunity to learn, create, and connect. Through hands-on workshops and meaningful experiences, we help members develop practical floral skills, express their creativity, and build lasting connections with others who share the same passion.
